This is my first post — a quick note on why this space exists and what to expect.

## Why a blog?

After a decade of building software products, I've accumulated a fair amount of opinions, lessons, and hard-won patterns. Most of them live in my head or scattered across Slack threads and internal docs that nobody will ever read again. This blog is an attempt to change that.

Writing forces clarity. If I can't explain something simply here, I probably don't understand it well enough to build it well either.

## What I'll write about

Mostly things I encounter in my day-to-day work as a product engineer:

- **Product thinking** — how to make better decisions about what to build and why
- **Frontend architecture** — design systems, component APIs, and the patterns that scale
- **Engineering leadership** — what I've learned about leading teams without losing touch with the code
- **Developer experience** — tooling, workflows, and the small things that compound over time

I won't follow a schedule. Posts will come when I have something worth saying.

## A note on tone

I'm not trying to write thought leadership or SEO-optimised listicles. These are notes from someone who builds things for a living — sometimes opinionated, sometimes exploratory, always honest.

If something here resonates or you disagree, I'd love to hear from you.
